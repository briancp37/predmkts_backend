### 2026-02-05: PnL Reconciliation Tests (Category: pnl_reconciliation)

Implemented comprehensive PnL reconciliation test suite verifying accuracy with known trade sequences:

**New test file:** `tests/test_gold_pnl_reconciliation.py` (20 tests)

Test coverage:
1. **Test Case 1 - Simple Buy-Sell Cycle:** Buy 100 @ $0.50, sell 100 @ $0.70 → verified $20 PnL
2. **Test Case 2 - Multiple Buys, Partial Sell:** Verified weighted average cost basis calculation
   - Buy 100 @ $0.50, Buy 50 @ $0.80 → avg_cost = $0.60
   - Sell 60 @ $0.75 → PnL = 60 * (0.75 - 0.60) = $9
3. **Test Case 3 - Position Flip:** Long to short via oversized sell
   - Buy 100 @ $0.50, Sell 150 @ $0.70 → close long PnL = $20, open short 50
   - Buy 50 @ $0.60 → close short PnL = $5
   - Total verified: $25
4. **Test Case 4 - Concurrent Positions Multiple Markets:** Same wallet trading in MKT_A (+$20) and MKT_B (-$10)
   - Verified separate position tracking
   - Verified aggregated wallet PnL = $10
5. **Ledger to wallet_pnl_daily Reconciliation:** Verified sum of per-fill realized_pnl_this_fill_usd equals wallet_pnl_daily total
   - Single wallet reconciliation
   - Multi-wallet reconciliation
   - Reconciliation with fees

Additional test coverage:

- Reopen position after close (fresh cost basis)
- Short position PnL (profit when price decreases)
- Edge cases: price at 0 (total loss), price at 1 (max profit), fractional quantities, breakeven trades, high fees turning profit to loss

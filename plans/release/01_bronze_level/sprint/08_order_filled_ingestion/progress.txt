## Notes
- Goldsky subgraph endpoint: https://api.goldsky.com/api/public/project_cl6mb8i9h0003e201j6li0diw/subgraphs/orderbook-subgraph/prod/gn
- OrderFilledEvent schema has 11 fields; source parquet has 8 (missing: orderHash, fee, id)

- Source parquet bucket: s3://polymarket-bcp892/raw/polymarket/order_filled/
- GoldskyClient does NOT use base_url on HttpClient (posts full URL) since it's a single endpoint, unlike REST clients

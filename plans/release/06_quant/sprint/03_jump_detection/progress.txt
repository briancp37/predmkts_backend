## Notes

Jump detection formula:
- sd_delta = sigma_b * sqrt(dt_years)
- threshold = m * sd_delta, m in [5, 10]
- is_jump if |dx| > threshold

Jump metrics:
- N_J: jump count
- lambda: N_J / T (intensity per year)
- E[z^2]: mean(jump_sizes^2)
- JV: sum(z^2) (jump quadratic variation)
- jump_share: JV / (RV + JV)

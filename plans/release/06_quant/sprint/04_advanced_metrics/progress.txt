## Notes

Corridor variance:
- Include dx only when p_{k-1} in [0.35, 0.65]
- Useful for "swing zone" volatility

Logistic Greeks:
- Delta_x = p(1-p): sensitivity of p to x
- Gamma_x = p(1-p)(1-2p): convexity

Martingale drift:
- mu = -0.5 * (1-2p) * sigma_b^2
- Ensures E[dp] = 0 (probability is martingale)

EWMA:
- alpha = 1 - exp(-ln(2)/halflife)
- EWMA[k] = alpha * RV[k] + (1-alpha) * EWMA[k-1]

## Architecture

Black-Scholes style quant analytics for prediction markets:
- Log-odds transformation: x = logit(p)
- Jump-diffusion model: dx = μdt + σ_b dW + dJ
- Belief volatility: diffusive component σ_b
- Jump detection: flag |dx| > m × σ_b × sqrt(dt)

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Interpretation |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| Realized Variance | RV = Σ(Δx)² | Total variation |
| Belief Volatility | σ_b = sqrt(TRV/T) | Diffusion vol |
| Jump Intensity | λ = N_J/T | Jumps/year |
| Logistic Delta | Δ = p(1-p) | Price sensitivity |
| Martingale Drift | μ = -½(1-2p)σ_b² | Risk-neutral drift |
